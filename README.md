# Neuquén Provincial Energy Balance

### Master's Thesis | Energy Economics and Environmental Policy

**Author:** Gustavo Alfredo Herrero  
**Study area:** Neuquén Province, Argentina  
**Base year:** 2013  
**Thesis completed:** 2016 | **Defended:** 2017

## 1. Project Overview

This research examines the energy system of Neuquén Province through the development of a provincial energy balance, with particular emphasis on the energy consumption of socioeconomic sectors.

The study integrates information on primary energy production, energy transformation, energy flows and final consumption, providing a comprehensive perspective on the province's energy structure and its role within Argentina's energy system.

## 2. Objectives

- Characterize Neuquén's provincial energy matrix.
- Analyze primary and secondary energy sources.
- Quantify energy supply, transformation and final consumption.
- Examine energy consumption across residential, transport, industrial, commercial, public and agricultural sectors.
- Identify structural characteristics and opportunities for energy planning.

## 3. Methodology

The research applies energy balance methodologies based primarily on OLADE and Argentina's national energy authority.

Information from multiple sources was collected, standardized and integrated into a consistent energy accounting framework.

Energy quantities were converted into a common unit, tonnes of oil equivalent (toe), enabling comparisons between different energy sources and sectors.

The study also includes a historical comparison with the provincial energy balance for 1985.

## 4. Key Findings

- **Primary energy production:** approximately 22.95 million toe in 2013.
- **Energy structure:** natural gas and crude oil dominated primary energy production.
- **Transport:** accounted for approximately 35% of final energy consumption.
- **Residential sector:** represented approximately 32%.
- **Industrial sector:** represented approximately 22%.
- **Energy profile:** Neuquén was a major energy-producing province, supplying energy beyond its internal requirements.
- **Structural challenges:** limited downstream industrial integration and low participation of non-conventional renewable energy sources in the 2013 matrix.

These findings refer to the 2013 base year and should not be interpreted as a description of the province's current energy structure.
